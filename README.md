**Barbershop Management Application** is a booking, scheduling, and customer loyalty platform tailored for premium barbershops and hair salons. It features real-time chair reservation calendars, barber roster scheduling, customer CRM, automated appointment reminders, and a Gemini-powered AI haircut consultant.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Customer Self-Service Booking Portal
- **Service Menu Selection**: Browse services (Classic Fade, Beard Sculpting, Hot Towel Shave, Keratin Treatment) with duration and pricing.
- **Barber Preference**: Choose preferred barber stylist with live availability slots.
- **Calendar Time-Slot Picker**: Instant booking without overlapping conflict locks.

### 2. Barber Chair Roster & Master Calendar
- High-density admin view displaying real-time daily queue, walk-in customers, active chairs, and revenue tally.
- Commission and tips breakdown per barber.

### 3. Gemini AI Hairstyle & Beard Consultant
- Upload face photo or specify face shape (Oval, Square, Round) to receive personalized haircut recommendations with style inspiration images.

### 4. Automated Notifications & Loyalty CRM
- WhatsApp & SMS appointment reminders sent automatically 2 hours prior to reservation.
- Customer visit history, hair cut preferences, and loyalty rewards points.
